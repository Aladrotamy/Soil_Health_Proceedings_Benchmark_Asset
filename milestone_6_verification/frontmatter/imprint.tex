\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Nature of this volume.} This volume is a demonstrative
publication prepared as a LaTeX benchmark asset. The symposium is fictional.
The contributed papers, their field trials, sites and datasets are synthetic:
every measurement is generated by a seeded computer program supplied with the
asset, and no value reports an experiment carried out on real land. The papers
cite real published literature for methods, definitions and context; those
works are cited, not reproduced. The results in this volume must not be used
as evidence about real soils, crops or farming systems.

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Conventions.} Quantities are given in SI units. Grain and
biomass yields are expressed in \si{\tonne\per\hectare} on a dry-matter basis
unless stated otherwise, soil organic carbon in \si{\gram\per\kilo\gram}, and
soil pH is measured in water at a soil-to-solution ratio of 1:2.5.
