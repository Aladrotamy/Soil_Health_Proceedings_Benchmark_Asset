\chapter{How This Volume Is Organised}

\section*{Structure}
The volume is divided into three parts. The front matter contains the title
pages, the contents, the editor's preface and this note. The main matter
contains the six contributed papers, one chapter each. The back matter
contains the list of abbreviations and the subject index.

\section*{The papers}
Each paper opens with its title, a short title used in the running head, and
an abstract. It then follows the usual order of a research paper:
introduction, materials and methods, results, discussion and conclusion.
References are listed at the end of each paper rather than collected at the
end of the volume, so that every paper can be cited and read on its own.

\section*{Data, tables and figures}
The data behind every table and figure are stored with the volume as
tab-separated files. Figures are drawn directly from those files when the
volume is typeset, and values quoted in the text are computed from the same
data, so text, tables and figures cannot disagree with one another.

\section*{Abbreviations and index}
Abbreviations are spelled out at their first use in each paper and are listed
together in the back matter. The subject index points to the pages on which
the main concepts, methods and measured properties are discussed.
