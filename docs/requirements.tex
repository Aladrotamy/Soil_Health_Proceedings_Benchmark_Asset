\section*{Task}
Prepare the \emph{Proceedings of the International Symposium on Soil Health and
Sustainable Crop Production, Volume~1} for publication by resolving the six
editor's queries recorded in \path{notes/review_log.md}, working only in the
Overleaf editor.

\section*{Required project content}
\begin{itemize}
  \item A proceedings volume with front matter, six contributed papers and back
    matter (List of Abbreviations and subject index).
  \item Each paper with title, abstract, keywords, introduction, materials and
    methods, results, discussion, conclusion and its own reference list.
  \item A synthetic, seeded dataset of 228 plots in five field trials at three
    sites, obeying 14 written data rules.
  \item Every table, figure and quoted number derived from the data through the
    results files; 25 qualitative statements consistent with the data.
  \item 18 references to published literature, each verified before use.
\end{itemize}

\section*{\LaTeX{} requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The project remains editable \LaTeX{} source.
  \item Papers are connected to \path{main.tex} with \path{subfiles}; each
    also compiles on its own.
  \item The volume compiles with pdf\LaTeX{}, biber and makeindex with 0 errors,
    0 warnings and 0 overfull or underfull boxes.
  \item Existing references are used consistently; no references are added.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Acceptance thresholds}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Verifier score exactly 1.0000 (\path{verifier/verify.py}): all six
    defects fixed and all seven gate checks passed.
  \item Page count 40--42; 64 untouched files identical to the golden solution.
  \item Land equivalent ratio of S3 in the Paper~3 conclusion equal to the
    computed 1.37 within $\pm 0.005$.
  \item All 9 columns of the sites table kept; no warning suppressed.
  \item Decoys \path{data/archive/t2_nitrogen_2025_export.tsv} and
    \path{bib/soilhealth_draft.bib} unchanged and unused.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Expected final state}
The compiled volume of \path{golden_solution/}, with the milestone history
kept in \path{milestone_1_foundation/} to
\path{milestone_7_final/} and \path{CHECKPOINTS.md}.
