\section{Results}

Only a modest fraction of the added carbon\index{carbon retention} remained in the topsoil after five
years (Table~\ref{tab:p5retention}). Averaged over the three amendments,
retention was \res{p5-ret-S2} at the sandy semi-arid site S2,
\res{p5-ret-S1} at the temperate site S1 and \res{p5-ret-S3} at the clay-rich
humid tropical site S3, so that it rose with the clay content of the soil
(Figure~\ref{fig:p5retention}).

\input{\subfix{../tables/p5_retention}}

Compost was retained most efficiently at every site, with fractions of
\res{p5-ret-S2-COMPOST}, \res{p5-ret-S1-COMPOST} and \res{p5-ret-S3-COMPOST} at
S2, S1 and S3. Manure was intermediate (\res{p5-ret-S2-MANURE},
\res{p5-ret-S1-MANURE} and \res{p5-ret-S3-MANURE}), and crop residues were
retained least (\res{p5-ret-S2-RESIDUE}, \res{p5-ret-S1-RESIDUE} and
\res{p5-ret-S3-RESIDUE}). The ranking of the amendments was the same at all
three sites.
